# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on code and ideas, not people.
- Assume positive intent and communicate clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
